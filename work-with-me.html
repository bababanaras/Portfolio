Work with me

Fractional design leadership and advisory for teams building AI products where the agent does the work and people stay in control.

I’m Head of Design at Property Vista, where I designed Vivian and Vida: time-to-lease down 60%, 92% of inquiries handled without staff, and 4.4x higher lead-to-appointment conversion across properties running Vida. I take on a small number of outside engagements, in Canada and the US, remote or hybrid.

Who it’s for

  • Founders and product leaders. shipping an AI agent or copilot and unsure how much it should decide on its own
  • Seed to Series C B2B SaaS. with no design leader yet, or one who needs a senior partner
  • PropTech, fintech and regulated products. where trust, compliance and handoffs to people are the product

Ways to work together

  • Fractional Head of Design2–3 days a week

    For seed to Series C teams that need senior design leadership before, or while, they hire full-time. I own design direction, set up the system and rituals, ship work myself, and help you hire my successor.

  • AI product design sprint2–4 weeks

    For one hard AI problem: an agent’s autonomy model, a human-in-the-loop handoff, an operator console. You get a tested prototype, the decision policy behind it, and a spec your engineers can build.

  • AI-native design practice4–6 weeks

    Set up the stack and the habits: Claude, Claude Code, Codex and Figma for concept-to-prototype in days, a design system with AI states, and synthetic plus solo user testing.

  • AdvisoryMonthly

    A standing session with your product and design leads: reviews of agent experiences, ROI stories for economic buyers, and the calls that are hard to make alone.

What you can expect

  • Measured. we agree the outcomes up front, from time-to-value to adoption
