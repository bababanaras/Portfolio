How I think

Principles for designing agents people can trust with a lease.

01

Autonomy is a dial, not a switch.

Set it per action, by stakes and reversibility. Not by what the model can technically do.

 02

Design the handoff before the happy path.

Trust is won or lost the moment an agent says "not mine to decide." Design that moment first.

 03

If it can't be undone, explain it first.

Reversible steps can move fast. Irreversible ones slow down, show their reasoning and wait.
